DOCUMENT SCANNING AND DIGITALIZATION SOLUTIONS

Document digitalization and management refers to the process of scanning paper documents and converting them in electronic documents, capturing important information and saving the document in a central repository to ease its further recovery. Replace the documentation storage spaces in your company. The maintenance and organization of those areas are usually difficult and imply a waste of your employees’ time. Migrate your physical files to a safe electronic store with a friendly search capacity. Save time, money and space and make a free-paper company.

WHAT THIS MEANS TO YOU?

If all your paper documents are scanned and stored in a central repository, yours can become a more organized, paper-free company where all important documentations are stored in no extra space. A good document management software will save your time and money by eliminating unproductive activities such as storing and recovering documents from binders and cabinets. MODOC provides tools to simplify the process of scanning and indexing large amounts of documents. You can split, merge and re-scan, set automatic classifiers by using bar codes or blank sheets for easy organization, filing and identification. Scanned files can be permanently saved in almost any format: Tif, JPG, PDF, DOC, XLS, MP3, AVI, etc. The OCR complement allows the identification of texts in scanned documents for an easy search and recovery by means of the MODOC document management search interface. By using metadata, you can also include data bases in our document indexation.

DIGITAL SIGNATURE AND DIGITAL SALARY RECEIPTS

It is a safe and reliable technological tool which allows to identify a document signatory and whether said document was falsified. It consists of a unique sequence of letters and numbers that link a signatory with an electronic document.

DIGITAL SIGNATURE: WHAT FOR?

It facilitates the replacement of paper documentation for its equivalent in digital format. As a cost-saving tool, it simplifies procedures and provides security in the exchange of information.

LEGAL VALIDITY

Electronic documents bearing a digital signature are as legally valid as those hand-signed.

•     Section 3 of Law 25.506
“When a document needs to bear a handwritten signature, a digital signature is valid as well.”

•     Section 288 of the Argentine Civil and Commercial Code
“Electronic documents where a person’s signature is requested can be digitally signed every time the document’s author and integrity are undoubtedly ensured.”
